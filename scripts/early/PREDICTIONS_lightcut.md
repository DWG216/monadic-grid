# Pre-registered before running lightcut.py
Question: does a region's local equilibrium pick out the conjugate shadow (lam' = -lam) at finite temperature?
P1 (fixed-time clock, flat weights): the best-matching shadow drifts away from -lam, and the drift grows as temperature falls (beta up).
P2 (light-cone clock, causal-diamond weights): the drift is at least 10x smaller than P1's at beta >= 2, and does not grow with beta.
P3 (controls: random smooth weights; inverted weights): no such improvement -- drift comparable to flat or worse.
FAILURE of the light-boundary idea for this test: P2 false, or a control does as well as the light-cone clock.
Known handicap, stated in advance: chirality tilts the light cones (right/left speeds differ); the light-cone weight uses the average speed, untilted.
