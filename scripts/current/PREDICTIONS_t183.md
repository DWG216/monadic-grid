# PREDICTIONS t183: the Tonnetz as the A₂ lattice; major and minor as the hand swap

Reading [I]. The Tonnetz places pitch classes on the hexagonal lattice A₂ (SU(3)'s weight lattice, the flower of life): lattice point (a, b) carries pitch class 7a + 4b mod 12 (fifths along one axis, major thirds along the other). Triads are its triangles. A triangle's orientation, pointing up or down, is the difference between SU(3)'s 3 and 3̄. The neo-Riemannian moves P, L, R reflect a triangle across one of its edges: an orientation flip, the hand swap. Transpositions translate the lattice: a change of key, the invisible phase (t182).

P1 [M]: the map has kernel generated by (0, 3) and (4, −1) (index 12): the Tonnetz is a torus with 12 vertices and 24 triangles; the 12 up-triangles are exactly the 12 major triads and the 12 down-triangles exactly the 12 minor triads, each once.
P2 [M]: P, L and R are involutions, each maps every major triad to a minor one and back (orientation reversed), and together they generate a group of order 24 that is dihedral (it contains an element of order 12).
P3 [M]: the orientation-preserving elements are exactly the 12 transpositions T_n (key changes), and every orientation-reversing element exchanges major and minor: the group is the 12 keys extended by the hand swap.
Consequence fixed now: if P1–P3 pass, the record holds: harmony's lattice is SU(3)'s, triad orientation is 3 against 3̄, key is phase, and the neo-Riemannian moves are the hand swap.
