# t141 The soloist: does will decide which way agreement lies? (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any t141 code is written or run)

Claim under test (theory doc, §267): the record fixes the direction of turning; will is only its strength, from 0 (refusing) to h/2 per beat; will decides how hard and when a mind joins, never which tone the shared agreement takes.
Setting: t135's two-law start (L = 24, r = 2, T = 600, three movements, whole monad carried, new places each movement), equal capacity a = 0.5 for all, majority fraction f = 0.75 on tone k1 with will 0.2; minority on tone k2 with will w_s. Seeds 41000–41009 per setting.
S1 [M] w_s = 0.2 (equal will): k1 in ≥ 9 of 10 (reproduces the numbers rule).
P1 [M] Soloists, w_s = 2.0 (ten times the will): k1 in ≥ 9 of 10: strong will does not carry the minority's tone.
P2 [M] Refusers, w_s = 0: k1 in ≥ 9 of 10.
P3 [M] Every world in every setting ends with one tone and median fraction complete ≥ 0.9.
Consequence fixed now: if S1, P1 and P2 pass, the claim stands: will sets how hard a mind joins, never which way agreement lies. If P1 fails (k2 wins), will can carry a tone, and the theory doc's statement of will's scope is withdrawn.
