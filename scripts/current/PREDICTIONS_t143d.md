# t143d A law holds without the will (pre-registered 2026-11-26, after t143c's first three worlds were seen, before any t143d run)

Why, from sufficient reason: a relation kept only because minds keep turning toward it, against their own frequencies, has its reason in the effort, not in the tones; when the effort stops, it should go. A law's reason is in the frequencies themselves: a resonant chord (ω_a + ω_b + ω_c = 0, exactly the cubic's terms, t136) needs no turning to stay. t143c's first worlds show the will holding about eight non-cubic chords for each cubic one; the test is what remains when the will is released.

Design: as t143c (η = 0, τ = 100, will 0.2, T = 600, rotation on), then the will set to 0 for 300 further beats (the record's average still running); locks counted at the end. Seeds 43000–43009.

P1 [M] At least one cubic chord is still locked in ≥ 7 of 10 worlds.
P2 [M] Cubic chords are at least half of the surviving locks in ≥ 7 of 10 worlds.
P3 [M] Summed over worlds, at least 80% of the cubic chords locked before release are still locked after (counted as cubic locks after / cubic locks at the end of t143c's matching run).

Consequence fixed now: if all pass, the chords that survive the will's release are the gauge structure's invariant: the will can hold any chord, but only the cubic holds itself. Law is what stays when no one holds it.
