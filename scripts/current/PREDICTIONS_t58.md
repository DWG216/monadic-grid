## A 3-adic tower: R_k = (Z/3^k)[phi]/(phi^2 - phi - 1), k = 1..4 (Galois rings; the limit is Z_3[phi], unramified)
A1 sigma: phi -> 1 - phi is a ring automorphism of R_k (checked on all products of a basis), sigma^2 = id.
A2 Teichmuller lift T = phi^(9^(k-1)) has T^8 = 1, and sigma(T) = T^3 (Frobenius = cubing on the 8 phase roots) at every k.
A3 Sigma(Z) = Z0 s(Z2) + Z2 s(Z0) + Z1 s(Z3) + Z3 s(Z1) is sigma-fixed ("real") for random Z at every k; Sigma(T^m Z) = T^(4m) Sigma(Z), T^4 = -1.
A4 Control: the principal unit u = 1 + 3 phi has sigma(u) != u^3 for k >= 2 (cubing is not conjugation off the phase roots).
## B Degrees and an explicit deformation of PT = PG(3,9)
B1 Helicity h has degree n = -2h-2; its swap -n-4. n = -n-4 mod 8 exactly for even h (h = 0, +-2): the two graviton helicities (degrees 2 and -6) share one degree class on PG(3,9); odd and half-integer h do not.
B2 Deformation (Penrose's patching on the overlap pi0 pi1 != 0) by f = omega0^2 (pi0 pi1)^8 (degree class 2): Phi(omega0, omega1, pi0, pi1) = (omega0, omega1 - 2 eps omega0 (pi0 pi1)^8, pi0, pi1), eps = 1. Phi is a well-defined bijection of the 820 points.
B3 Deformed curves C_x = Phi(L_x), L_x = {(x pi, pi)}, x in M2(F9) (6561 points): each has 10 points; each twistor point off pi = 0 lies on 81 of them.
