The antisymmetric part of 27⊗27, the 351, couples only to quarks and only to off-diagonal entries, with opposite sign on 1-2 and 2-1. With quark-only antisymmetric weight x (in units of the lepton 1-2 entry): M_12 = (r12 + x) a, M_21 = (r12 − x) a for down quarks, a for leptons. Enumerated: every (r22, r12) of t114 with x scanned; reported is the x the data (s/μ 0.231, d/e 2.44 at M*) require, and whether a simple Clebsch (x = 0, 1/2, 2/3, 1) lies within 10%. Exact singular values checked against the leading order.
