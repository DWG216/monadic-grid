# t85 Where E6 breaks: the roadmap's item (5) (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any t85 code is written or run)

Ground (not on trial): the cut E8 -> E6 x SU(3) gives three generations (t60); the cut E6 -> SU(3)^3 gives the forces, and the couplings meet there at M3 = 4.48e13 GeV, alpha^-1 = 38.76 (t67, t70); equal Dynkin index keeps the three couplings equal above M3 (t68 G2); E6's own 54 bosons are the only gauge bosons that turn quarks into leptons (t68 G1); colour-triplet scalars sit at 5.6e11-7.5e13 GeV (t71 G3).

Bridge under test [S]: M_E6 is not a free scale. Running cannot fix it (equal indices make it invisible to the couplings); a scale fixed by nothing would be an unexplained choice. The grid has two scale-setting structures: a cut (the meeting of the couplings, M3) and the meeting term of gravity (the reduced Planck mass, 2.435e18 GeV). M3 is excluded for E6's bosons by Super-K (t68). So E6's 54 bosons sit at the gravitational meeting: M_E6 = reduced Planck mass.

Lifetime estimate as t68: tau = M^4 / (c^2 m_p^5), m_p = 0.938 GeV, Super-K tau(p -> e+ pi0) > 2.4e34 yr; Hyper-K reach taken as 1e35 yr (about 20 years of running).

S1 Sanity: the t68 bound is reproduced: alpha^-1 = 42.4 gives 4.64e15 GeV.
G1 [M] Perturbative up to the gravitational meeting: running alpha^-1 from 38.76 at M3 with b = -5 + n_h (t71 G1), n_h = 0..3, up to 2.435e18 GeV stays above 1 for every n_h.
G2 [M] E6 boson decay is invisible: with M = 2.435e18 GeV and alpha from G1, tau(p -> e+ pi0) > 1e40 yr for every n_h (five orders beyond Hyper-K).
G3 [M] Scalar decay is where any signal lives: with c = y_u y_d (t68 G5), the triplet window 5.6e11-7.5e13 GeV gives a lifetime range whose lower end is above the Super-K bound and whose upper end is beyond Hyper-K; reported: the triplet mass below which Hyper-K (1e35 yr) sees p decay.
C1 Control: E6 bosons at M3 (4.48e13 GeV, alpha^-1 = 38.76) give tau below the Super-K bound (excluded), reproducing why M3 is ruled out.
C2 Control: an E6 scale at the usual SU(5)-type unification (2e16 GeV) gives tau between 1e35 and 1e40 yr: the prediction differs observably from the standard one.

Verdict: all pass -> the grid predicts no gauge-boson proton decay at Hyper-K; any p -> e+ pi0 or nu pi+ it sees must come from colour-triplet scalars below the G3 reach mass. A Hyper-K signal with the gauge-boson branching pattern (e+ pi0 dominant, no K+) at 1e35 yr would break this bridge.
