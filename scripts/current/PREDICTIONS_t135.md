# t135 Which law the Community holds: development or numbers (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any t135 code is written or run)

Setting: t134's variant B (whole monad carried, new spacetime each Age; t128 dynamics, L = 24, r = 2, T = 600, three Ages), started from two laws in place of noise. A majority (fraction f) holds law k1: attention 0.5 on k1, the rest uniform; phases at k1 aligned (common phase plus noise 0.3). A minority (1 − f) holds law k2 the same way. Positions random. Seeds 35000–35009 per setting. The Community's law = the single law held at the end of Age 3 (k1, k2, or other).
Corpus reading under test: development leads (more distinct minds act, Leibniz §49–52; Gods guide the Collective).
S1 [M] Equal capacity (a = 0.5 for all), f = 0.75: k1 (the majority law) wins in ≥ 9 of 10.
P1 [M, the corpus reading] Developed minority (minority a = 1.0, majority a = 0.05), f = 0.6: k2 wins in ≥ 7 of 10.
P2 [M, same] f = 0.75: k2 wins in ≥ 7 of 10.
P3 [M] f = 0.9: k1 wins in ≥ 7 of 10 (numbers overwhelm development at large enough odds).
P4 [M] Every world in every setting ends with one law and median fraction complete ≥ 0.9 (the Community forms whichever law it holds).
Writer's note: the dynamics give developed monads both stronger turning (κ·a) and stronger listening (w^a), so they may conform faster rather than lead; P1–P2 can fail. Consequence fixed now: if P1 and P2 pass, development decides the Community's law below a threshold of numbers (P3 locates it); if they fail, numbers decide and the corpus reading is dropped for these dynamics.
