# t134 The Community of Gods across Ages: the whole monad carried (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any t134 code is written or run)

Reading under test (user, 2026-11-25): the Community of Gods is the first end state of t126/t128: one law that every monad holds completely; the many local laws of large worlds are a stage of coarsening, not an end. Distinctness among Gods with identical content is kept by the phase, which no record reads (|e^{iφ}f|² = |f|²).
Change from t121/t128: there, only capacity crossed an Age (attention and phases redrawn). A monad's attention p and phases φ are its own, not the record's (corpus: part of the soul keeps the memory of every life, record §172). Here the whole monad crosses: capacity a, attention p, phases φ. What closes is spacetime: each new Age places the monads in a new spacetime, i.e. on fresh, randomly permuted grid positions (new neighbours).
Model: t128_community.py's dynamics unchanged (local record, Chebyshev r = 2, will 0.2, T = 600 per Age, η = 0.3); law, distinct laws (held by ≥ 5) and complete (a ≥ 0.5, C > 0.8, holding a law) as t128. Seeds 34000–34009. Five Ages.
Variants: B (whole monad kept, positions reshuffled); C (whole monad kept, positions kept: one long spacetime with breaks only in the noise schedule); A (capacity only kept, as t128 P4).
S1 [M] A reproduces t128 P4's picture in the large world (L = 24): laws ≥ 5 in every Age, median fraction complete at Age 5 below 0.5.
P1 [M] B, large world (L = 24): at Age 5, at most 2 laws in ≥ 7 of 10 worlds, and median fraction complete ≥ 0.8.
P2 [M] B, small world (L = 12): one law by Age 2 in ≥ 7 of 10 worlds.
P3 [M] B, large world: the number of laws does not rise from Age 1 to Age 5 in any world, and the median fraction complete rises Age on Age (non-decreasing medians).
P4 [M, blind; writer's expectation] C, large world: coarsening continues across Ages without reshuffling but slower than B: median laws at Age 5 above B's, median fraction complete at Age 5 below B's.
Consequence fixed now: if P1–P3 pass, the Community of Gods is derived: carried across Ages in new spacetimes, every monad comes to hold one law completely, each distinct by its phase. If P1 fails, the many-laws state survives whole-monad Ages and the Community stays open.
