# t62 does the carrier (time arrow) pick the forward count w over w^2? (pre-registered 2026-11-23, before run)
Setting: SL(2,3) = 2T (the 24-cell group, mod 3); the counting step U = [[1,1],[0,1]] (order 3); its 1-dim characters w, w^2
(w(U) = e^{2 pi i/3}) label matter and antimatter (§143). The record's golden time step Q = [[0,1],[1,1]] (det -1); the hand swap = complex conjugation of representation values.
C1 SL(2,3) has two classes of order-3 elements (4 each); U and U^-1 are in different classes, distinguished by w.
C2 Conjugation by Q (det -1) swaps the two classes: w(Q g Q^-1) = w(g)^2 for all g. Conjugation by Q^2 (det 1) preserves w.
C3 Conjugation by Q^-1 also swaps: reversing the arrow's direction does not change which way w goes.
C4 Complex conjugation (hand swap) sends w to w^2.
C5 The golden step with its hand swap (conjugation by Q composed with complex conjugation) preserves w for all g.
Expected reading if C1-C5 hold: the carrier conserves chirality (matter stays matter step after step) but its direction does not create it; the initial choice of w is not fixed by the arrow alone.
