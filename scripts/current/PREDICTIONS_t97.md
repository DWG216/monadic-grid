# t97 Which axis the neutrinos keep: the residuals must make the whole group (pre-registered 2026-11-25, before any t97 code is written or run)

Status of the criterion, stated plainly: it is chosen after t96's post hoc showed S4 offers a TM1 axis and a theta13 = 0 axis. The test here is (a) whether the criterion picks exactly one class, fixed by the group and not by data, and (b) the predictions that follow for data not yet taken (JUNO's full precision, the theta23-delta relation, DUNE and Hyper-K's delta).

Criterion [S]: the family group is S4 = PGL(2,3) (the grain's 2T plus the hand swap, t96 post hoc). A symmetry no sector keeps would play no part and have no reason to exist, so the residual symmetries of the two sectors must together generate the whole group: <Z3 (Dirac, the counting phase T), Z2 (Majorana)> = S4.

S1 Sanity: <T, S> with S in A4 generates A4 (order 12), not S4.
P1 [M] Among the order-2 elements of S4 outside A4 (the 6 transposition-class rotations), exactly those whose axis is not orthogonal to T's axis generate S4 with T; those orthogonal to it generate S3 (order 6). Exactly one class (up to conjugation by <T>) survives.
P2 [M] The surviving class gives a PMNS column with moduli (2/3, 1/6, 1/6): TM1.
P3 [M on data] TM1's sum rule sin^2 theta12 = 1 - 2/(3(1 - sin^2 theta13)) = 0.318 (sin^2 theta13 = 0.02215) lies within 2 sigma of JUNO (0.3092 +- 0.0087).
P4 [M] TM1's relation cos delta = -(1 - 5 s13^2) cot(2 theta23) / (2 sqrt2 s13 sqrt(1 - 3 s13^2)) has a solution (|cos delta| <= 1) for sin^2 theta23 anywhere in NuFIT 6.0's 1-sigma ranges (0.455-0.573, union of the two published fits).
Information: delta predicted at sin^2 theta23 = 0.470 and 0.561 (both signs), against NuFIT's 212 deg.
Verdict: P1-P4 pass -> the grid's flavour group with its own hand swap gives TM1: sin^2 theta12 = 0.318 (JUNO will settle it at the percent level) and delta tied to theta23 (DUNE, Hyper-K).
