# PREDICTIONS t188: triads: three voices meeting

Registered 2026-11-28, committed before t188_triads.py is written or run. Disclosure: the arithmetic for the named chords is simple and was worked out while designing; the derivation is the content, and P4 states the one structural claim.

Derivation [S], extending t186–t187. Three tones in the ratio a : b : c (integers, no common factor) leave a record that repeats when all three return together, after a periods of the lowest; counted in their geometric-mean period, with no voice privileged, that is D₃ = (abc)^{1/3}, the three-voice form of √(pq). Each note's ratio to the root is the grid's own (t187: the simplest five-limit point of its Tonnetz coset).
Two grid readings tested here: (i) the hand swap in frequency: the major triad is a stretch of the overtone series (4 : 5 : 6), the minor triad its mirror in the undertones (1/6 : 1/5 : 1/4 = 10 : 12 : 15); (ii) a three-voice resonance: frequencies with a + c = 2b have equal difference tones, the relation ω_a − 2ω_b + ω_c = 0, of the same kind as the octave chord (k, k, 2k) locked in every world (t138) and the three-voice chords of the cubic (t136, t140).

P1 [M]: the major triad's integers are 4 : 5 : 6 and the minor's 10 : 12 : 15, the minor being the major's reciprocal (undertone) mirror; D₃(major) < D₃(minor).
P2 [M]: among the six common triads, D₃ orders them major < suspended fourth < suspended second < minor < augmented < diminished.
P3 [literature, qualitative]: the major triad is more consonant than the minor, and both more than the augmented and the diminished (the consistent finding across listening studies, e.g. Johnson-Laird et al. 2012, Bowling et al. 2018); P2's order agrees with this.
P4 [M]: among all 55 root-position triads {0, x, y} (0 < x < y < 12), the ones with D₃ < 5 are exactly those satisfying the three-voice resonance a + c = 2b.
Consequence fixed now: if P1–P4 pass, the record holds: the most consonant three-voice chords are the three-voice resonances; major and minor are the hand swap in frequency (overtones and undertones), and the record prefers the overtone side.
