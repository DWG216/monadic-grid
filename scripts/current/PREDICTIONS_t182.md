# PREDICTIONS t182: music theory from the record (balanced chords, the record's blindness, consonance)

Registered 2026-11-28, committed before t182_music.py is written or run.

Dictionary [S]. A pitch class k of the 12-note octave is the unit phase e^{2πik/12}; a chord is a sum of them. Transposing a chord by j multiplies its Fourier coefficients by phases e^{−2πijm/12}, so the record |f̂_m|² cannot hear the key: key is the monad's invisible phase (|e^{iφ}f|² = |f|²), and the record hears only intervals. A balanced chord is one whose unit phases sum to zero. Two partials that coincide exactly give a phase-free meeting term; partials that nearly coincide give beating cross terms.

P1 [M]: every balanced subset of the 12 pitch classes is a disjoint union of tritones {k, k+6} (the balanced pair) and augmented triads {k, k+4, k+8} (the clock of three), the only balancing parts allowed by 12 = 2² · 3 (Lam–Leung); the count of balanced subsets is reported.
P2 [M]: the record (|f̂_m|², m = 0..11, of the pitch-class indicator) separates the set classes of 12-tone music (sets up to transposition and inversion) except exactly for the Z-related (homometric) pairs; their number is 23 (1 tetrachord, 3 pentachord, 15 hexachord, 3 septachord, 1 octachord pairs), with no Z-triples.
P3 [M]: consonance as the share of the record that is meeting, not beating: two harmonic tones (8 partials, amplitudes 1/n) at a just ratio p/q; C = Σ over coinciding partial pairs of a_i a_j divided by Σ over all pairs of a_i a_j. Prediction: the top four are unison, octave, fifth, fourth, in that order, and the minor second (16/15) is last among the twelve intervals (just ratios 16/15, 9/8, 6/5, 5/4, 4/3, 45/32, 3/2, 8/5, 5/3, 16/9, 15/8, 2/1).
Consequence fixed now: if P1–P3 pass, the record's music theory is: balance builds chords from pairs and the clock of three; the record hears intervals and not keys, and is blind exactly to homometry; consonance is the share of meeting terms.
