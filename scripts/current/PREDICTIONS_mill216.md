# Pre-registration (2026-11-21): Millington pp. 216-225
M1 [id] Epilogue 3: F(2X-3) = F(X)F(X-1) - F(X-2)F(X-3) holds for every integer X (not only even X), including negative X; it is F(X-1)^2 + F(X-2)^2 = F(2X-3).
M2 [id] Even formulas: F(n) = F(n/2) L(n/2) and F(n) = F(n/2+1)^2 - F(n/2-1)^2 for even n (positive and negative).
M3 [real] Epilogue 4: spin-corrected (balanced, -4..4) Fibonacci mod 9 has period 24 with F(n+12) = -F(n); Lucas mod 9 likewise period 24 with L(n+12) = -L(n).
M4 [id] Epilogue 5: dividing by 2 mod 9 = multiplying by 5 (2*5 = 10 = F(4)^2 + 1, Cassini F(3)F(5) = F(4)^2 + 1). General: F(n-1)F(n+1) = (-1)^n mod F(n)^2.
M5 [id] Halved pair (x, y) = (L(n)/2, F(n)/2) mod 9 satisfies x^2 - 5y^2 = (-1)^n mod 9: Millington's divide-by-2 maps the strands onto the grid's domain curve (section 12).
M6 [id] Catalan: F(n)^2 - F(n-r)F(n+r) = (-1)^(n-r) F(r)^2 for all n, r in [-30, 30].
