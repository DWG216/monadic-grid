## t136 Resonant chords of the 27 are E6's cubic (exact)

S1 [M] The 27 weights are distinct and nonzero (minuscule).
P1 [M] The unordered triples of distinct states with zero weight sum are exactly the 45 monomials of the E6 cubic I₃ = det A + det B + det C − tr(ABC) (3 × 6 determinant terms + 27 trace terms), no more and no fewer.
P2 [M] Under a random generic frequency map (1,000 draws of v ∈ R⁶, ω = v·weight), frequency resonance selects the same 45 triples every time.
Consequence: if P1–P2 pass, chords that form by resonance on E6's 27 tones are the terms of E6's cubic: the record's third-order invariant is what third-order minds can lock.
