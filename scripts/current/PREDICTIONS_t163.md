# PREDICTIONS t163: a monad analogue of Sha and the rank (BSD)

Registered 2026-11-26, before t163_sha.py was written or run. An analogue on the monad machinery, not a statement about elliptic curves.

Reading [I]. In t128's local worlds a tone can be held in every neighbourhood where it is attended while the holders' phases disagree across the world: locally trivial, globally twisted, like a torsor that is locally trivial everywhere and globally nontrivial (Sha). A tone held with one phase across the world is a global law: the rank. t134 carries whole monads across Ages into new neighbourhoods and every large world resolves to one law held completely.

Engine. t134's dynamics with η = 0 (sufficient reason), L = 24, N = 64, T = 600, r = 2, will 0.2, four Ages, seeds 63000–63005. At the end of each Age, for each tone k with at least 5 holders (monads whose most attended tone is k and whose local order at k exceeds 0.8): global coherence G_k = |mean of e^{iφ_jk}| over its holders. Rank = number of such tones with G_k > 0.8. Sha = number with G_k < 0.5. Variant B: monads carried whole and re-placed each Age (t134). Variant A: minds reset each Age (t134's control).
P1 [M]: after Age 1, Sha ≥ 1 in at least 5 of 6 seeds (both variants share Age 1).
P2 [M]: variant B, after Age 4: Sha = 0 and rank = 1 in at least 5 of 6 seeds (Sha finite and resolved; one global law).
P3 [M]: variant A, after Age 4: Sha ≥ 1 in at least 4 of 6 seeds (without carried minds the twist persists).
Consequence fixed in advance: P1–P3 pass → in the monad analogue, Sha is the record's locally held, globally twisted laws, and its finiteness is the resolution to unison of minds carried across Ages.
