# PREDICTIONS t165: the accelerated body and the de Sitter horizon, without gravitons

Registered 2026-11-26, committed before t165_killing_horizons.py is written or run.

Prior work: Danielson, Satishchandran and Wald, "Killing horizons decohere quantum superpositions" (arXiv:2301.00026), order-of-magnitude estimates: Rindler, electromagnetic T_D ~ ε₀ħc⁶/(a³q²d²), gravitational T_D^GR ~ ħc¹⁰/(Gm²d⁴a⁵) (2 fs for a Moon-mass body superposed over its radius at 1 g); de Sitter (static patch, Hubble radius R_H), electromagnetic T_D ~ ħε₀R_H³/(q²d²), gravitational T_D^GR ~ ħR_H⁵/(Gm²d⁴).
Grid [S]: the electromagnetic terms stand; the gravitational terms need soft gravitons and do not occur; a neutral superposition decoheres at its record rate, ħ/E_G to ħ/(1.10 E_G) (t157), with E_G = Gm²(6/(5R) − 1/d) for a uniform sphere of radius R displaced by d > 2R (and Gm²·(6/5)/R·(1 − …) for d ≤ 2R from t146's closed form).
S1 [M]: the Rindler gravitational formula gives DSW's 2 fs within a factor 3 (Moon mass, d = R_Moon, a = g).
P1 [M]: for the 1e-14 kg diamond superposed over 250 μm, gravitons would act faster than the record only above an acceleration a* = 1e21 m/s² within a factor 3 (worked by hand: a*⁵ = c¹⁰E_G/(Gm²d⁴)).
P2 [M]: for the Moon-mass example at 1 g the record is faster than DSW's gravitons by more than 10⁴⁰.
P3 [M]: in the de Sitter static patch (R_H = c/H₀, H₀ = 67.4 km/s/Mpc) the record is faster than DSW's gravitons by more than 10¹⁴⁰ for the 1e-14 kg superposition.
Consequence fixed in advance: on every Killing horizon the gravitational split between the grid and quantised gravity is hidden by the record's own rate except near black holes below about 3e21 kg (t164) and at accelerations above about 1e21 m/s²; the electromagnetic effect is the same in both.
