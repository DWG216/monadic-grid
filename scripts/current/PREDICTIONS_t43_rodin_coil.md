# t43 what the Rodin coil bench will say (pre-registered 2026-11-23 00:30)
Model: air-core torus, major radius 40 mm, minor radius 15 mm, 36 slots. Each wire makes poloidal loops at the slots whose residue mod 9 is 1, 2, 4, 8, 7, 5 (Millington: 3, 6, 9 are gaps), visited in the doubling order, joined by short runs along the outer equator. Wire B = wire A shifted by k slots, lying 0.5 mm outside it. Mutual inductance by the Neumann double integral.
R1 [M] The doubling path's jumps (+1, +2, +4, -1, -2, -4 slots) cancel over each 9-slot block: no net azimuthal advance, so the winding has no one-turn component (balanced).
R2 [M] M(k) is periodic in k with period 3 (slot set invariant under a 3-slot shift), to < 1%.
R3 [M] M(1) = M(2) to < 1% (slot set symmetric under negation): the bench shows two coupling values per period, not three.
R4 [M] Counter-wound (B reversed): M changes sign exactly; series-opposing inductance L_A + L_B - 2|M| is smallest at k = 0 mod 3.
R5 [S] No output exceeds input: the coupling coefficient |k_c| = |M| / L stays below 1.
