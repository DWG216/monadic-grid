Closed-Winding Particle Catalogue

The book's picture of the universe is a frequency domain of light outside spacetime, a six-dimensional grid of three real and three imaginary axes, and matter generated holographically from a null point. This page writes each of those ideas as mathematics, checks it against the Standard Model, and builds the particle catalogue on top of it.

Part 2 · The six-dimensional frequency domain

Three complex axes give colour

Pair each real (cosine) axis with an imaginary (sine) axis and the book's 6D grid becomes three complex planes. Rotating those planes into one another while keeping complex volume fixed is exactly the group SU(3). Checked numerically:

Quark

a wave along one complex axis

The three axes are the three colours. A gluon is one of the 8 independent ways to mix the axes.

120° winding

det(e2πi/3·I) = 1

Turning all three complex planes by 120° at once keeps the volume fixed. That is the colour step in the closure rule.

Baryon and meson

z₁∧z₂∧z₃ and z̄·z unchanged

Three quarks, one per axis, span a complex volume. A quark and antiquark form a complex length. Both are colourless.

The hypercharge phase is the Euler formula's own phase, turning in 60° steps. Weak isospin has no home in this geometry and is added as a two-component label.

Part 3 · Stability rules

Closure and anomaly freedom

1. Closure

2t + 3d + 6Y ≡ 0 (mod 6)

Colour (120° steps), isospin (180°) and hypercharge (60°) windings must add up to whole turns. In the book's terms, a configuration has to return to itself, like the perfectly orthogonal pair in a photon. This is the real ℤ6 structure of the Standard Model gauge group.

2. Anomaly freedom

SU(3)³, SU(3)²U(1), SU(2)²U(1), U(1)³, grav²U(1), Witten

A set of chiral configurations must cancel all six quantum anomalies, or the theory breaks down. This rule decides which configurations can exist together.

Closure also forces charge quantisation. Every closed colourless state has whole-number charge, and every closed colour triplet has charge 2/3 or −1/3 modulo 1.

Part 4 · Mind becomes matter

Before the Higgs settles, everything is light-like

In the unbroken theory, every particle in the catalogue is massless. Left-handed and right-handed fermions have different windings, so an ordinary mass term can't close. In the book's language, the whole catalogue starts in the frequency domain, on null lines.

Matter appears when a left-handed piece is paired with a right-handed piece through the Higgs. This plays the role of the book's "non-orthogonal recombination." The search over all such pairings finds exactly four that close:

The windings decide which pairings are allowed. They say nothing about how strong each pairing is, so the masses stay as inputs.

Part 5 · The null point and the hologram

Spacetime from frequency

Every field in the catalogue is an inverse Fourier transform: a sum of frequency modes, each spread over all of spacetime. That is the true part of the book's "whole in every part." It doesn't allow faster-than-light signalling.

In the frequency plane below, light-like modes lie on the null cone through the origin. Massive modes lie on hyperbolas pushed away from it. The origin itself, zero frequency and zero momentum, holds one thing: the Higgs condensate, a constant field filling all of spacetime. It is the coupling to this zero-frequency mode that moves particles off the null cone.

After the Higgs settles, only one combination of W³ and B leaves the vacuum unchanged: Q = T₃ + Y, acting as −1/2 + 1/2 = 0. That combination is the photon, which stays massless. The gluons stay massless because colour is never broken. So the frequency domain ends up holding exactly light and the gluons. Everything else, including the W, Z and Higgs, is matter.

What this does and does not show

What the formalised picture gets right

  • Three complex axes give colour SU(3) exactly, including the 120° step and colourless baryons and mesons.
  • Closure picks out 13 configurations and forces charge to come in thirds.
  • Anomaly freedom picks out exactly one Standard Model generation.
  • The mind/matter split matches massless versus massive. The frequency domain ends up holding only light and the gluons.
  • Matter comes from pairing two chiral pieces through a zero-frequency condensate. Exactly four pairings close.

What it leaves open or gets wrong

  • All 12 fermion masses, the boson masses, the mixing angles and the three force strengths are inputs.
  • Why there are three generations.
  • Weak isospin and the left-handedness of the weak force come from nowhere in the book's geometry.
  • Three literal time dimensions conflict with observation. They only work here as internal axes.
  • Spin is assumed. The cosine/sine account of bosons and fermions doesn't hold up.

The book's 60° step survives as the unit of hypercharge winding. Its 45° and doubled-frequency guesses play no part. Where the formalised picture works, it is because the reinterpretations were chosen to land on known physics, not because the choran and chronon picture derives it.
