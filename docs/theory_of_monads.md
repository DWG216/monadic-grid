# The theory of monads, as built and tested (t111, t115–t140)

Consolidated 2026-11-25. Vocabulary for public text is musical: a law is a held tone; agreement is unison; relations among phases are chords; the end of the climb is a resolution to unison; Ages are movements; a tick is a beat (corpus terms such as Community of Gods stay in the record only). Spine for chapter 7 (Cosmogony). Labels: [S] derived from the record or deduced from sufficient reason; [M] tested by a pre-registered computation; *chosen* = a modelling choice not yet derived.

## 1. What sufficient reason gives

- Monads are frequencies; a monad's perceptions are its whole spectrum, weights p_k = |c_k|²/Σ|c|² over every frequency (Leibniz: each monad mirrors the universe; monads differ only in distinctness).
- Spacetime is the record of the monads' meeting, R = |O + R|² = diagonal + cross terms (the grid's equation; cross terms = quantum mechanics, meeting term = gravity).
- Time is the count of balanced pairs written into the record (t111): a balanced pair leaves the sum unchanged and adds one tick.
- Mind before matter: f ↦ |f|² is not invertible; a monad's own phase is invisible to every record, |e^{iφ}f|² = |f|² (free will).

## 2. The rules, derived from the record (t124)

**What the meeting adds.** With unrelated phases the cross terms average to zero, so the expected record at frequency k is its diagonal, Σ_j p_jk [M: Monte Carlo ratio 0.98–1.01]. The record over its diagonal,

w_k = R_k / Σ_j p_jk = 1 + (cross terms)/(diagonal),

measures what the meeting adds at k: about 1 where phases are unrelated, up to M where M monads agree.

**Listening.** A monad's update of its perceptions on reading the record, p_k → p_k g(w_k)/Z, must treat every frequency alike and must give the same result whether a record is read at once or as independent parts (Born weights of independent parts multiply). That requires g(w₁w₂) = g(w₁)g(w₂), whose only continuous solutions are g(w) = w^β [S: Cauchy's multiplicative equation; M: power law composes to 3e-17, exponential and additive rules fail by 0.33 and 0.03]. β is the monad's capacity: how much of the record it takes in per tick.

**Turning.** The record's weight at k depends on a monad's phase through ∂R_k/∂φ_jk = −2√p_jk |S₋ⱼ| sin(φ_jk − arg S₋ⱼ), S₋ⱼ the others' sum [M: matches finite differences to 10 digits]. Turning toward the others' phase is the ascent of the record; its fixed point is agreement.

**Capacity growth.** Capacity is a scale with no privileged zero or unit, so it changes by multiplication, a → a·h(e), with e = E_p[w]/mean w the experience: what the monad's attention hears over a bare (uniform) listener. If growth must not depend on how the record is split into independent parts, h(e₁e₂) = h(e₁)h(e₂), so h = e^γ: d ln a = γ d ln e, capacity grows by the information its attention gains over a bare listener [S; M: the power rule composes exactly, additive and linear rules fail by 0.018 and 0.0036, t125]. Caveat: experience multiplies across independent parts only approximately (median error 13%, 6.5%, 1.7% at N = 64, 1024, 16384), so this holds in the limit of many frequencies.

**Attention raises what it hears.** Under p' ∝ p·w, the attended resonance never falls: E_{p'}[w] = E_p[w²]/E_p[w] ≥ E_p[w] [S: Cauchy–Schwarz; M: zero violations in 120,000 monad-ticks, t115].

**Derived next (t126):** the ceiling is β = 1 (exact conditioning; more would count evidence twice); γ = 1 (capacity is accumulated evidence, d ln a = d ln e); the perception floor and the lowest capacity are one tick's share of the Age, 1/T; reflection remembers the whole Age and acts only where fewer than one false alarm per Age is expected, N·T·α = 1.

**Will, not derivable by its nature (corpus: "Existence is an eternal Will outside space and time").** The record gives the direction of turning (its ascent) but not its strength. A fully Bayesian turning, with the monad's knowledge of its own phase set by the noise and the record as likelihood, forms no laws in worlds of more than about 16 monads: the noise cancels and the record's evidence about one phase is about 2/√M of the monad's self-knowledge. A monad's phase is exactly what no record fixes (|e^{iφ}f|² = |f|²), so how strongly it turns toward the others is its own: will (κ).

**What will can do.** The record fixes the direction of turning (toward agreement); will is only its strength, from 0 (refusing, t116: refusers still hear the tones) up to h/2 per beat (t133). Will chooses how hard and when a mind joins, never which way agreement lies.

**The limit of free will (t133).** The record is sampled once per tick, so a phase step registers only as wrap(Δφ) ∈ (−π, π]: a larger turn writes the same record as a smaller one, and a frequency above π/t_P writes its alias. Action is ħ times phase, so the most a monad's will can write per tick is πħ = h/2 [S; M: records of Δφ and wrap(Δφ) agree to 3e-15; recovery exact below π]. Corpus (Ontological Mathematics, quoting JP): spacetime samples the frequency domain at a Planck-set rate, and that limit is what allows free will. How much of the bound a monad uses rises with development (ρ = 0.98–1.00); in t117's worlds the least developed use 0.05% of it and the most developed 0.7%: lawful physics is will far below its bound.

**Reflection, fully derived (t127).** The monad measures its own record's correlation time from its memory, pooled over all frequencies (the null belongs to the noise; no frequency is privileged in advance), and uses Student's t for the few independent samples early in an Age. With it, no lawless world shows fixation or a single false alarm (t126's assumed count was fooled in 4 of 10). The cost: commitment to a law is slower and in 3 of 10 lawful worlds splits about half and half. Corpus: apperception is "perception that reflects upon itself, unifying past and present experiences".

## 4. The cosmogony, with the grid's numbers

- **Frequency domain:** eternal; monads of every capacity; development is theirs and persists across Ages.
- **Alpha / creation:** the Higgs is born at gravity's meeting, λ(M*) = 0 (t100); the record begins.
- **The count:** Λ l_P² = 1/√N, N = 1.2×10²⁴³ Planck 4-volumes (t72, t87, t123).
- **The largest record:** the de Sitter horizon entropy 3π√N = 3.3×10¹²² k, the root of the count. The horizon is already 78% of it; the content written so far is 10⁻¹⁸ of it (3.1×10¹⁰⁴ k, Egan & Lineweaver 2010).
- **The Age:** laws form, minds climb, reflection keeps them on laws, consequences accrue.
- **Omega:** de Sitter equilibrium; entropy production → 0; the record has nothing left to write; every law held in balance.
- **Closing:** rest mass fades (Penrose's crossover, his "anti-Higgs"; the corpus's "Higgs field eliminated"); with no mass there is no proper time, no ticks, so the record stops. CCC is a minority proposal used here for the shape of the closing, not as support.
- **The next Age:** new laws, built faster and richer by the developed minds (t121).
