Flavour from the grain's symmetry, S₄

What this solves

Why do neutrinos mix so much and quarks so little? The grain's symmetry S₄ fixes the pattern, and the residual symmetries leave one mixing family: TM1.

S₄ = 2T + the hand swap
sin²θ₁₂ = 0.318, δ ≈ 262°–286°

S₄ = PGL(2, 3) is the tetrahedral group plus the swap of the two hands (φ → 1 − φ). Its residual symmetries must generate the whole group, which leaves TM1.

What TM1 fixes

The solar angle at 0.318 from the reactor angle, and the CP phase in 262°–286°.

Against the data

TM2 sits 3.6σ from JUNO's solar angle; TM1 fits.

Foundations: the two hands

Every frequency comes as a pair, e+iωt and e−iωt: two hands. The symmetry that exchanges them is the hand swap.

Foundations: and the quarks?

Both quark sectors keep the same Z₃, so their mixing starts at zero and stays small.
