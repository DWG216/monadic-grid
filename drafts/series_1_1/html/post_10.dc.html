Dark matter is a light singlet

What this solves

Dark matter must be stable, neutral and abundant. The grid's breaking leaves a parity under which every new particle of the 27 is odd: the lightest of them cannot decay.

ψ parity
P = (−1)Qψ + F

Breaking the extra U(1)ψ with charge-2 vevs leaves P: +1 on every particle of the 16, −1 on the 11 extras. The lightest extra is stable.

Its mass

A singlet of ψ charge 4 gets MN = vψ⁴ / M*³: between 0.015 and 21 GeV.

Why a singlet

Z exchange on nuclei rules out the doublets. The parity leaves one stable particle: a light singlet.

Foundations: why stable?

P is a label, +1 or −1, that every vev and every 27³ coupling keeps. The lightest particle labelled −1 has nothing to decay into.

Foundations: why light?

Four powers of the breaking over three of gravity's meeting: a high scale makes a light particle.
