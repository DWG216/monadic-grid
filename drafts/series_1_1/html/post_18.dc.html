Time is the count of balanced pairs

What this solves

What is time, if the laws do not mention it? A balanced pair leaves the world unchanged and adds one tick to a count: time is that count.

A null constructor
eiθ + ei(θ+π) = 0

Adding a balanced pair changes nothing in the sum and one in the count. Deutsch and Marletto (2026) define time through such null constructors; the grid supplies them.

The arrow

The record |S|² falls at half of all steps; the count rises at every one. The arrow is the count.

Why it cannot run back

Squaring forgets the phase: from the record, the pairs cannot be unwritten.
