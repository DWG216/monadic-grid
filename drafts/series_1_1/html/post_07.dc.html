E6 breaks where gravity meets

What this solves

Unification needs one scale where the larger symmetry breaks. In the grid it is gravity's own meeting: the reduced Planck mass, where the Higgs is also born.

One meeting
M* = (8πG)−1/2 = 2.435 × 10¹⁸ GeV

E6 breaks to three SU(3)s at gravity's meeting, the scale where the meeting term of the record becomes gravity. The cut to the Standard Model follows lower, at about 5 × 10¹³ GeV.

What it gives

No proton decay through E6's gauge forces: they sit at the Planck scale.

What else sits there

The Higgs self-coupling starts from zero at M* (post 14), and the ψ charge's breaking sets the dark sector's scale (posts 10–11).

Foundations: why there?

Two scales with no reason between them would be an unexplained choice; the grid has one, gravity's meeting, and E6 breaks at it.
