Neutrinos take the charged leptons' masses

What this solves

Neutrino masses need a Dirac mass and a heavy Majorana partner. In the grid the Dirac masses are the charged leptons' own, which fixes the heavy partners.

The seesaw
mν = mD² / MR, mD = mℓ

With the charged leptons' masses as Dirac masses, the measured splittings fix the heavy partners at 7.2 × 10⁵, 1.3 × 10⁹ and 6.3 × 10¹⁰ GeV.

What follows

Normal ordering; the sum of the three masses 59.16 meV; neutrinoless double beta decay at 1.2–4.1 meV.

A route closed

With these partners, the standard route to the matter excess through their decays falls short. The excess comes another way (post 11).
