The hidden mirror

What this solves

The sharpest objection to E8 unification is that it cannot be chiral. The theorem holds for the grid; its mirror family is gapped by interaction, which ties the mirror to the breaking of family symmetry.

The theorem
248 = (78, 1) + (1, 8) + (27, 3) + (27, 3)

Distler and Garibaldi (2010): no embedding in E8 gives a chiral theory. Three generations arrive as (27, 3) + (27, 3): each family with its mirror, net chiral index 0.

The answer

The mirror is gapped by interaction, not by a mass term. It carries no Standard Model anomaly and Z₁₆ index 0, so nothing forbids it.

The price

Family SU(3) has anomaly 27 on the mirror, so the mirror gaps only where family symmetry breaks: mirror gapping and family masses come together.

Foundations: does this evade the theorem?

It steps outside its assumptions: the theorem is about representations, and gapping by interaction is not one.

Foundations: where the mirror goes

It is gapped where family symmetry breaks: the same place the family masses are made.
