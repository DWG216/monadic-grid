Dark energy does not flicker

What this solves

Dark energy does not flicker. It is the exact, constant leftover of a fixed count. Our random-walk version failed DESI's distances and is withdrawn; the constant fits them.

A fixed count
Λ lP² = 1/√N, N ≈ 1.2 × 10²⁴³

Λ is what is left over from a fixed count of Planck four-volumes. The count's total is fixed, so Λ does not change: w = −1 exactly.

The distances

A constant Λ fits DESI DR2's baryon acoustic distances (p = 0.41), and the count gives its size within a factor of 2.

The constant, not the walk

Post 46 let Λ wander as a random walk. A fixed count has no walk in it: its remainder is constant.

Foundations: why constant?

A change in time would need a reason the count does not supply: a fixed total leaves a fixed remainder.
