The scalar vacuum needs two Higgs 27s

What this solves

Breaking SU(3)³ to the Standard Model needs the right scalars. One Higgs 27 leaves an extra symmetry unbroken; two leave exactly SU(2) × U(1).

The zero rule's reach

With all twenty quartics of the two 27s zero at M*, the potential at the cut is unbounded. The rule applies to the light Higgs's own self-coupling.
