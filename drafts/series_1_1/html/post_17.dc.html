What this solves

The light Higgs's charges occur twice in a pair's record: in the leptons and in the quarks. The cubic takes (+1, −1); the orthogonal half takes the only other direction, lepton 3, quark 2.

Two jobs

Foundations: why orthogonal?

A pair's record splits into parts that do not mix, like perpendicular directions: two halves, no third, the second fixed by being orthogonal to the first.
